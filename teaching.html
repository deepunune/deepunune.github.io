Undergraduate & postgraduate courses

Teaching

Over fifteen years of teaching undergraduate and postgraduate engineers, my approach has settled on three things: be clear, stay curious, and keep the theory tied to something that exists in a workshop. I bring live case studies, the software students will actually be handed on the job, and results from my own laboratory into the classroom.

Courses

ME223 · Even semester · Programme core

Machining and Metal Forming

The fundamentals of removing and deforming material: turning, milling, drilling, forging, rolling and extrusion. The emphasis is on process mechanics, tool design and surface integrity, and on analysing what each process parameter actually changes.

MME305 · Odd semester · Programme core

CAD/CAM

How computer-aided design and manufacturing fit together in a real workflow. Students work hands-on in SolidWorks or Fusion 360 and cover CNC programming, geometric modelling and automated manufacturing systems.

Even semester · Open elective

Management of Production and Operations Systems

Running a production system efficiently: operations strategy, forecasting, inventory control, lean manufacturing, scheduling and quality management, worked through to data-driven decisions.

Odd semester · Programme elective

Unconventional Machining Processes

The processes that do not use a cutting edge — electrical discharge machining and its wire and micro variants, electrochemical machining, ultrasonic and abrasive jet machining, and laser and electron beam processes — with attention to where each one is the right answer and what it costs in rate, accuracy and surface integrity. This is the course closest to my own research.

Laboratories

Alongside lectures I have run and built laboratory courses in manufacturing, CAD/CAM, and measurement and control, and establishing new laboratory infrastructure has been part of every academic post I have held at LNMIIT.

Workshops

I have run hands-on software workshops for students, including SolidWorks (2023) and Fusion 360 (2024).
